# Context-aware mRNA design: evidence-first manuscript outline

## Research question

Can RNA representations predict vaccine-relevant regulatory properties under
controlled assay identity and context, without equating reporter signals with
vaccine protection?

## Results sequence

1. Regulatory atlas and assay/readout provenance (Figure 1).
2. Translation/reporter versus stability prediction (Figure 2).
3. Sequence x Cell x Time decomposition and legal OOD (Figure 3).
4. Matched pretraining/adaptation benefits and costs (Figure 4).
5. Same-construct joint outcomes; Poly(A) only with measured evidence (Figure 5).
6. Independent multi-objective selection and reproducibility (Figure 6).

## Claim boundary

No formal admitted tasks, no universal ranking, no causal RBP/miRNA mechanism,
no fabricated Poly(A) labels and no demonstrated vaccine effectiveness. Local
exploratory CPU results require source, noise and rights review before Results.
Only at most three validated questions may become primary scientific claims.
